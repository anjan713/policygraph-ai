Policy Summary: Prior Authorization Guidelines

Prior Authorization
Prior authorization is required for advanced imaging, selected specialty medications, and out-of-network procedures.
A request is eligible for review when provider documentation includes diagnosis, requested procedure, clinical notes, and medical necessity.
Requests missing provider documentation require manual review.

Decision Rules
Services are covered when medical necessity criteria are met and the requested service is listed as eligible under the member policy.
Services are not covered when policy exclusions apply or documentation is insufficient after review.
